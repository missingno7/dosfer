DOSfer legacy-clean video modes
================================

The sender is intentionally fixed to QR Version 40 at one VGA pixel per QR
module. Only the 320x200 planar Mode 0Dh backend remains.

/VIDEO:320_60  (default)
    BIOS VGA mode 0Dh plus custom ~59.94 Hz vertical timing. The memory
    layout stays 320x200, 16-color planar, with the original 8 KiB page
    stride and double buffering.

/VIDEO:320_70
    Unmodified BIOS mode 0Dh timing at approximately 70 Hz.

The removed 640x480 backend and /SCALE option were legacy paths that were no
longer used by the V40 transfer setup. Keeping one raster geometry also makes
the stateful codeword-to-pixel delta map much simpler and easier to verify.
